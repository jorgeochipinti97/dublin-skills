# UGC Ad Angles — Skeletons

Each angle is a structural commitment. One angle per script. Never mix. Load only the angle the user requested.

## Testimonial
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — concrete result in first sentence ("Bajé 6kg en 8 semanas sin dejar de salir a comer")
2. **Context (3-8s)** — who I am / what I tried before
3. **Discovery (8-18s)** — how I found the product, one specific feature that cambió todo
4. **Proof (18-25s)** — numeric result + timeline
5. **CTA (25-30s)** — direct action + urgency if real

Best for: consumer products with real customers, skincare, fitness, apps with measurable outcomes.

## Problem-Solution
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — name the pain, specific ("Si tu skincare te arde cuando sudás...")
2. **Agitation (3-8s)** — what makes it worse
3. **Old way fails (8-14s)** — why lo que probaste no funcionó
4. **Solution (14-22s)** — product + unique mechanism in plain words
5. **CTA (22-30s)**

Best for: categories with obvious pain, SaaS, tools, supplements, problem-aware audiences.

## Founder
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — "Armé este producto porque [specific frustration]"
2. **Origin (3-12s)** — the moment, not the arc
3. **What we do different (12-22s)** — one concrete mechanism
4. **Invitation (22-30s)** — low-friction CTA ("Probalo y decime qué te parece")

Best for: new brands, DTC launches, founder-led audiences, community building.

## Reaction
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — visible reaction on camera ("No puede ser que esto funcione así")
2. **What I just tried (3-10s)** — product in use, show don't tell
3. **What surprised me (10-20s)** — specific moment
4. **Recommendation (20-30s)** — for whom it's for

Best for: viral-style on TikTok, discovery products, unboxing-adjacent.

## Demo
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — show the end result first ("Mirá cómo queda en 30 segundos")
2. **Setup (3-8s)** — problem/starting point
3. **Steps (8-22s)** — 3 quick beats, on-screen text per step
4. **Result + CTA (22-30s)**

Best for: software, tools, kitchen gadgets, hair/beauty with visible transformation.

## Before-After
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — the after, not the before. Reversed reveal.
2. **Before context (3-10s)** — what was wrong, timeline
3. **What I did (10-20s)** — product + routine
4. **After + CTA (20-30s)** — result with date/timeline

Best for: fitness, skincare, hair, teeth, home transformation. Needs real visual change.

## Comparison
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — "Probé [competitor popular] y [ours]. Esto pasó."
2. **Criteria (3-8s)** — 3 axes of comparison
3. **Winner per axis (8-22s)** — honest, including where competitor wins
4. **Overall verdict + CTA (22-30s)**

Best for: crowded categories, product-aware audiences, price-conscious segments.

## Myth-Bust
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — the myth stated as fact, then disagreement
2. **Why people believe it (3-8s)**
3. **What's actually true (8-22s)** — with evidence
4. **Implication + CTA (22-30s)**

Best for: health/wellness, finance, niches with widespread misinformation.

## POV
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — "POV: [specific scene]"
2. **Beat (3-25s)** — the scene plays out, product appears naturally
3. **Tagline + CTA (25-30s)**

Best for: lifestyle, fashion, beauty, moments-based positioning. TikTok-native.

## List
1. **Hook (0-3s)** — "5 cosas que hubiera querido saber antes de [X]"
2. **Items (3-25s)** — on-screen numbered, fast cuts
3. **Final item = product (or product as wrap) + CTA (25-30s)**

Best for: educational content, content-first brands, newsletter/course funnels.

## Selection Heuristics

| If you have... | Best angles |
|---|---|
| Real customer results | Testimonial, Before-After, Comparison |
| No proof yet | Founder, POV, Myth-Bust, List |
| Visual transformation | Demo, Before-After |
| Crowded competitor space | Comparison, Myth-Bust |
| New category / novel product | Founder, Problem-Solution |
| Content-led brand | List, Myth-Bust, POV |
| Pain-aware audience | Problem-Solution, Testimonial |
| Unaware audience | POV, List, Myth-Bust |
