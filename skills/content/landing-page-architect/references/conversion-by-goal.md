# Conversion Playbooks by Goal

Per-goal playbook. Load the section matching the user's goal. Each playbook = structure + hero pattern + key sections + proof strategy + CTA pattern + what to avoid.

---

## 1. Direct Sales (B2C or B2B, transactional)

**Commitment**: high. Long-form.

**Structure**: Hero → Problem/Agitation → Solution + Unique Mechanism → Features/Benefits (deep) → Deep Social Proof → Offer + Anchoring → Guarantee → FAQ → Urgency → Final CTA.

**Hero pattern**: outcome headline + specific timeframe or result + price-anchoring subhead + direct "Buy" CTA.

**Proof**: testimonials with specific numbers · before/after · case studies · money-back guarantee prominent.

**CTA**: action + outcome — *"Get instant access"*, *"Claim my spot"*, *"Start my transformation"*.

**Levers**: price anchoring (show the "real" price crossed out, current price) · bonuses stacked · risk reversal · real urgency (cohort close, price increase, bonus expiration).

**Avoid**: multiple plans above the fold · vague "contact us" · hiding the price if price ≤ $2k.

## 2. Lead Generation (capture email for nurture/sales)

**Commitment**: low. Short to medium.

**Structure**: Hero with lead magnet → Benefits of the magnet (3-4 bullets) → Social proof (users of the magnet) → Form (email only) → Secondary objection killer → CTA.

**Hero pattern**: *"Get [specific lead magnet] that [specific outcome]"* + preview of the asset.

**Lead magnet rules** (from copywriting-theory §12): specific, fast, proven, relevant, tangible. Bad: *"Ultimate marketing guide"*. Good: *"The cold email script that booked 47 demos last month"*.

**Proof**: user count · one testimonial using the magnet · preview of what's inside.

**CTA**: *"Send me the [asset]"*, *"Get my free [asset]"*. Never "Submit".

**Form**: email only. Phone/name/company = massive friction drop. Ask later in the nurture flow.

**Avoid**: asking for phone/company in the first form · generic guide titles · no preview of the asset · promising revenue outcomes ("earn $10k/month") — screams scam.

## 3. Waitlist (pre-launch, beta, invite-only)

**Commitment**: very low. Very short.

**Structure**: Hero → 1-2 sentence "why this exists" → Micro-proof (founders, investors, or early waitlist count) → Form (email + optional "tell us about yourself") → Perks of joining early.

**Hero pattern**: big idea + exclusivity + countdown or "early access" framing.

**Levers**: FOMO (real) · exclusivity · referral mechanism ("move up the list by referring") · early-user perks · founder credibility.

**Proof**: *"2,400 people ahead of you"* · investor/company logos · founder pedigree.

**CTA**: *"Get early access"*, *"Join the waitlist"*, *"Save my spot"*.

**Avoid**: too much product detail (mystery is the point) · asking for money · promising dates you won't hit · big forms.

## 4. Webinar / Event Registration

**Commitment**: low (registration) to medium (showing up).

**Structure**: Hero (date/time prominent) → Speaker bio with credibility → What you'll learn (3-5 outcomes) → Agenda → Testimonials from past webinars → Register form (email + name) → Urgency (seats or time).

**Hero pattern**: *"[Date, time, timezone] — [Outcome the attendee will get]"* + speaker photo.

**Proof**: speaker credentials · past attendee count · testimonials with quotes from past events · recordings available ("or watch the replay").

**CTA**: *"Save my seat"*, *"Register for free"*. Include calendar add-to-calendar link after signup.

**Levers**: limited seats (real) · early-bird bonus · live-only content · Q&A with speaker · recording offered only to attendees.

**Avoid**: burying the date/time · generic "learn about X" agendas · no speaker credibility · forms with 6+ fields.

## 5. App Download (iOS / Android / desktop)

**Commitment**: low to medium.

**Structure**: Hero (app name + one-line promise + store badges) → Key features (3-4 with phone mockups) → Screenshots gallery → Reviews/ratings → Privacy/trust note → Download CTA.

**Hero pattern**: big phone mockup + short headline + App Store + Google Play badges side by side.

**Proof**: star rating with review count ("4.8 ⭐ · 12,000 reviews") · featured-in badges ("Featured on App Store") · user count · press quotes.

**CTA**: the store badges themselves. Also a "see it in action" video trigger.

**Smart detection**: show only the relevant store badge based on device (iOS → App Store, Android → Play, desktop → both or QR code).

**Avoid**: tiny store badges · no screenshots · no rating · walls of feature text · asking for email before downloading.

## 6. SaaS Free Trial (PLG signup)

**Commitment**: medium.

**Structure**: Hero → Social proof bar (logos) → Problem/outcome → Product demo (screenshot/video) → Features → benefits (3-6) → Integrations (if relevant) → Deep proof (case studies) → Pricing table → FAQ → Final CTA.

**Hero pattern**: outcome headline + "Start free" CTA + "No credit card" trust line + product screenshot or video.

**Proof**: customer logos bar (immediately after hero) · product screenshots · 2-3 testimonials · case study with metric · G2/Capterra rating.

**CTA**: *"Start free"*, *"Try it free"*, *"Get started free"*. Trust line: *"No credit card · 14-day trial · Cancel anytime"*.

**Levers**: no credit card required (vs. card-on-file = ~3× signup, ~30% lower quality) · self-serve pricing transparent · instant onboarding · integration logos for category fit.

**Avoid**: hiding pricing · forcing demo for product below $500/mo · asking credit card upfront unless intentional · showing "contact sales" for a self-serve product.

---

## 7. Newsletter Subscription

**Commitment**: very low. Very short.

**Structure**: Hero (what the newsletter gives + frequency) → Preview (one sample issue or topic list) → Who reads it (subscriber count + notable names if any) → Email form → Past issues link.

**Hero pattern**: *"Get [specific benefit] every [frequency]"* + preview of what a subscriber gets.

**Proof**: subscriber count · notable subscribers · ratings · past issue previews · testimonials.

**CTA**: *"Subscribe"*, *"Get the next issue"*, *"Join [count] subscribers"*.

**Levers**: specific benefit per issue (not "industry insights" — be concrete) · frequency transparent (weekly, daily, monthly) · "unsubscribe anytime" reassurance.

**Avoid**: generic benefits ("stay up to date") · no preview of content · hiding frequency · heavy forms.

---

## 8. In-Person / Virtual Event Registration

**Commitment**: medium (paid events) or high (in-person travel).

**Structure**: Hero (event name, date, location) → Why attend (3-5 bullet outcomes) → Speakers/agenda → Past event highlights (photos, testimonials) → Pricing tiers (early bird, regular, VIP) → FAQ → Register CTA.

**Hero pattern**: event name + date + location + hero visual (past event photo or speaker lineup) + register CTA.

**Proof**: past event photos · speaker credibility · sponsor logos · attendee quotes · attendee count.

**CTA**: *"Register now"*, *"Get my ticket"*, *"Reserve my seat"*.

**Levers**: early-bird pricing (real) · limited capacity · VIP tier anchoring · bundle offers.

**Avoid**: no clear date/location · no speaker info · only one pricing tier · burying the venue logistics.

---

## 9. Book a Demo (B2B sales-led)

**Commitment**: medium to high.

**Structure**: Hero (outcome + "book a demo" CTA) → Logos bar → Who it's for (ICP clarity) → Case studies with metrics → What you'll see in the demo (agenda) → Calendar embed or qualifying form → FAQ → Final CTA.

**Hero pattern**: outcome headline for a specific ICP + *"Book a 30-min demo"* CTA + calendar preview.

**Proof**: enterprise logos · case studies with $ impact · G2 ratings · security/compliance badges · ROI calculator (if relevant).

**CTA**: *"Book a 30-min demo"*, *"See a personalized demo"*, *"Get a walkthrough"*. Embed calendar (Calendly, Chili Piper) to reduce friction.

**Form fields** (if qualifying): work email · company · company size · use case. Nothing else.

**Levers**: personalization ("tailored to your stack") · short demo length (30 min max) · no pressure ("no sales pitch") · case studies matching the visitor's segment.

**Avoid**: forms with 10+ fields · "talk to sales" language (cold) · no ICP clarity · no case studies · calendar buried behind a form with phone required.

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Goal | Length | Form size | Risk reversal | Price shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct sales | Long | Card on file | Strong guarantee | Yes |
| Lead gen | Short | Email only | — | No (free asset) |
| Waitlist | Very short | Email only | — | No |
| Webinar | Short-medium | Email + name | — | Usually free |
| App download | Medium | None (store) | Store reviews | Store |
| SaaS trial | Medium | Email + password | No credit card | Yes (transparent) |
| Newsletter | Very short | Email only | Unsubscribe anytime | Free |
| Event | Medium | Email + name + company | Refund policy | Yes (tiered) |
| Demo | Medium | Work email + company | — | "Custom" okay for enterprise |
