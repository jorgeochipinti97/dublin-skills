# Copywriting Theory

Dense reference for direct-response landing page copy. Sources: Schwartz, Ogilvy, Hopkins, Halbert, Sugarman, Cialdini, Copyhackers, NN/g, CXL.

---

## 1. Schwartz's Two Axes

Plot the prospect on BOTH before writing.

### 1.1 Awareness (what they know)

| Level | Knows | Headline must |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Nothing | Big idea, story, identity — never product |
| Problem-aware | Pain | Name the pain viscerally |
| Solution-aware | Solutions exist | Outcome + new mechanism |
| Product-aware | Your product | Proof + differentiation + sweetener |
| Most-aware | Ready to buy | Offer + price + guarantee |

Cold traffic on a "Buy Now" page = burned budget.

### 1.2 Sophistication (how tired the market is)

| Stage | Condition | What works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First claim | "X does Y" |
| 2 | Competitors exist | Bigger, more specific claims |
| 3 | Claims are tired | Introduce a **mechanism** |
| 4 | Mechanisms copied | New angle, elaborate the mechanism |
| 5 | Everything tried | Identification — tribe, movement, lifestyle |

Most 2026 B2B SaaS and consumer markets are stage 4-5. "Boost productivity" is stage-1 copy in a stage-5 market. It loses.

## 2. Headlines

Ogilvy: 5× more people read headlines than body. 80% of the work is the headline.

### Tests (the 4 U's)
Useful · Urgent · Unique · Ultra-specific. Hit 3/4 = good enough. 4/4 = rare.

### Formulas
- Outcome + timeframe: *"Close books in 4 hours, not 3 days."*
- Big promise + "without": *"Fluent Spanish in 6 months without grammar drills."*
- Before/After identity: *"From overwhelmed founder to booked-out CEO in 90 days."*
- Question hitting a pain: *"What if your CRM knew what your customers wanted?"*
- "How I…": *"How I tripled my MRR by firing 70% of my clients."*
- Negative framing: *"Stop writing blog posts nobody reads."*
- New mechanism: *"The reverse funnel that turns cold leads into paying customers."*

### Anti-patterns
Vague benefits · generic category claims · empty superlatives · fake urgency · clever over clear.

### Subheadline's job (pick ONE)
Clarify the "who" · clarify the "how" · add credibility · remove an objection.

## 3. Frameworks (decision tree)

- **PAS** (Problem → Agitate → Solution) — cold, emotional pain
- **BAB** (Before → After → Bridge) — visualize transformation
- **FAB** (Feature → Advantage → Benefit) — warm, feature-rich SaaS
- **AIDA** (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action) — compressed, hot audience
- **StoryBrand** (7 parts: hero/user, problem, guide/product, plan, CTA, stakes, transformation) — narrative brands
- **QUEST** (Qualify → Understand → Educate → Stimulate → Transition) — B2B, research-driven buyers
- **4Ps** (Picture → Promise → Proof → Push) — punchy, visual brands

Quick rule:
- Cold + emotional → PAS / BAB
- Warm + B2B SaaS → FAB / StoryBrand
- Hot → AIDA, lead with offer

## 4. Features → Benefits

Prospects buy outcomes. Run every feature through "so what?" × 3.

| Feature | Advantage | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| 256-bit encryption | Data unreadable to attackers | Sleep knowing a breach won't end your career |
| React Server Components | Less JS in browser | Site loads <1s — users don't bounce |
| 1-click rollback | Undo deploys instantly | Ship Friday afternoons without fear |

---

## 5. CTAs

Formula: `action verb + value/outcome`. Never just the action.

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Submit | Get my free audit |
| Sign up | Start my 14-day trial |
| Buy now | Claim my spot |
| Learn more | See how it works (90 sec) |
| Register | Save my seat |

Rules: ONE primary CTA · repeated 3-5× · same text/color · above the fold · trust line under it (*"No credit card · Cancel anytime"*).

---

## 6. Social Proof (ranked by power)

1. Specific results with names — *"40 → 180 demos/mo in 9 weeks — Maria Chen, Head of Sales, Acme"*
2. Video testimonials with faces
3. Written quotes with name/role/company/photo
4. Case studies with metrics + arc
5. Logos of recognizable brands
6. Numbers (users, downloads, uptime)
7. Press / media mentions
8. Review scores (G2, TrustPilot)
9. Awards, certifications

Placement: micro-proof after hero · macro-proof mid-page · contextual near features · pre-CTA reinforcement.

---

## 7. Risk Reversal

The #1 lever for high-commitment offers. Shift the risk from buyer to seller.

- Money-back (30/60/365 days — longer = stronger)
- Free trial — no-credit-card vs card-on-file (trade conversion for quality)
- Freemium — prove value first
- Outcome-based — "pay when you succeed"
- "Unconditional" language — "No questions asked"

Pattern: *"If [product] doesn't [outcome] in 30 days, we refund every cent — and you keep the bonuses."*

---

## 8. Objection Handling

Top 5 universal:
1. Too expensive → ROI, comparison, guarantee, payment plans
2. Won't work for me → case studies from similar situations
3. No time to implement → time-to-value, onboarding support
4. Don't trust you → proof, guarantees, about page, transparency
5. Can get it elsewhere → unique mechanism, differentiation

Where: inline when it arises · FAQ for leftovers · near CTA (quick reassurance) · guarantee (covers price/trust).

## 9. Voice & Rhythm

### By audience
- **Engineers**: precise, evidence-led, zero hype
- **Founders**: direct, confident, time-aware
- **Consumers**: warm, conversational, aspirational
- **Luxury**: restraint, confidence, understatement
- **Enterprise**: formal, credible, risk-aware

### Rhythm
- Vary sentence length. Short. Short. Longer sentence that breathes. Short.
- One idea per paragraph
- Bucket brigades: *"Here's the thing:"* / *"But there's a catch:"* / *"Now watch:"*
- Read it aloud — if you stumble, they stumble

### Banned words (vendehumo)
revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, next-gen, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, synergy, leverage, unlock, supercharge, 10x, ninja, rockstar, guru, unprecedented, paradigm shift, ecosystem, solution, seamless, frictionless, effortless, thought leader, actionable insights, robust.

---

## 10. Urgency / Scarcity — ONLY when real

- Cohort closes (real deadline)
- Limited seats (real inventory)
- Price increase (real change)
- Bonus expires (real bonus)
- Event-tied ("Q2 cohort April 15")

Never: fake countdown timers, "only 3 left" lies, evergreen "limited time" banners.

---

## 11. Message Match

Ad → landing page headlines must mirror. Mismatch = 30-50% conversion drop.

---

## 12. Lead Magnets (lead gen only)

Specific, fast (<10 min to use), proven, relevant, tangible.

Bad: *"The Ultimate Guide to Marketing"*
Good: *"The cold email script that booked 47 demos for our agency last month"*

---

## 13. Length

Length = commitment level. Not a target.

- Low commitment (newsletter, waitlist, free tool) → short, one screen okay
- Medium (trial, demo, low-ticket) → 5-8 sections
- High (high-ticket, B2B, paid course) → 10-20 sections, every objection answered

Ask: "Did I answer every question before they're ready to click?"

---

## 14. Writing Process

1. Write body first, extract headline last
2. 10 headlines, pick one
3. Read aloud
4. Cut 30%
5. Replace vague words with concrete ones
6. "You" : "I/we" ratio ≥ 3:1
7. Remove jargon unless technical audience

---

## Quick Filter

Before finalizing, check:
- Headline specific + useful + unique + urgent?
- "So what?" × 3 answered?
- ONE primary CTA?
- Top 3 objections addressed?
- Proof near every claim?
- Risk reversed?
- Reads like one human talking to another?
- Every sentence earning its place?
