
```

```

```
comic10 con esta nota: https://tribuna.ar/articulos/...
```

```
Vamos con otro reel que no sea de Boca
```

```
Hacé un reel papercut de esta nota de la Selección, con la cara de Scaloni (sale en la foto de la nota)
```

## Example Use Cases

- Turning a sports news article into a 25-33 s vertical reel for Instagram/TikTok
- Injury/comeback stories with the player's real face as a paper cutout
- Match previews and fixture news (possible rivals shown as "posible")
- National-team news (no faces invented: an empty #10 shirt stands for a player not in the article)
- Same recipe for another brand (e.g. a livestock app) by swapping identity and cast in theme.tsx
